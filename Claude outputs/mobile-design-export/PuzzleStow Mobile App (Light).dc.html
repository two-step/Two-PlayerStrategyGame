Support

Steps (≤ 3)

\n

\n
Overhang & Fair Play

\n
\n
\n
\n
\n
\n
\n
\n

More than 50% of a piece's area left unsupported after placement triggers a bilateral fair-play confirmation — both players must accept before the move registers, or nothing is placed.

How to Actually Win
\n
\n
Keep rows in play as long as possible — avoid creating dead cells.
